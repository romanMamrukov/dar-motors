# Customer test plan — maximum 10 minutes

Do not show code first. Give the device to the employee.

1. **0:00–1:30 — Public inventory.** Ask them to open Cars, find one vehicle and explain what they understand from the card.
2. **1:30–3:00 — Vehicle page.** Ask where they would look for price, mileage, preparation and contact action.
3. **3:00–6:00 — Add a vehicle.** Open `/admin/cars/new`, then say only: **“Add a car yourself.”** Do not explain fields unless they are blocked for >30 seconds. Ask them to upload 2–3 photos and publish as Available.
4. **6:00–7:00 — Verify public result.** Return to Cars and find the newly added vehicle.
5. **7:00–8:00 — Edit.** Ask them to change the price and save.
6. **8:00–9:00 — Sale workflow.** Ask them to mark the vehicle Reserved, then Sold. Verify the public badge changes.
7. **9:00–10:00 — Enquiry.** Submit one public enquiry and verify it appears under Admin → Leads.

## Observe, do not coach
Record: hesitation points, fields they skip, labels they misunderstand, device used, time to first published vehicle, and any request to return to Facebook/SS.com workflow.

## Pass criteria
- Employee publishes a car in <= 4 minutes with no instruction.
- Employee changes price/status without assistance.
- Customer understands why this is more useful than a static corporate website.
