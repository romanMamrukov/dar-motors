# D.A.R. Motors customer feedback

Record answers verbatim before proposing features.

1. What is inconvenient right now?
2. What feels unnecessary?
3. What is missing to use this weekly?
4. Who will add and update cars?
5. Which device will they normally use?
6. How many cars are normally for sale simultaneously?
7. Which vehicle fields are mandatory in your real process?
8. Where are cars advertised today?
9. Should this system eventually publish anywhere else automatically?
10. Which languages are required: Latvian, Russian, English?
11. Do you want website enquiries, or are calls/WhatsApp enough?
12. Is Reserved a useful status?
13. Should Sold vehicles remain publicly visible as history?
14. What statistics would change a business decision?
15. What would make you pay for this rather than continue the current process?
16. What monthly or one-time price would feel reasonable after it saves you time?
